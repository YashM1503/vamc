# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in VAMC respectful and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept moderation decisions and correction gracefully.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when publicly
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban a participant, depending on severity and
pattern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; conflicts of
interest will be disclosed and routed to a neutral reviewer when possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
